# PRO V7 Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Upgrade the supplied game collection with playable bots, adventure powers, premium mobile UI and persistent rewards.
**Architecture:** Retain pure rules and DOM/SVG boards; add focused adventure and bot modules plus one shared experience layer. Extend the existing progression source and account actions.
**Tech Stack:** JavaScript ES modules, CSS, SVG, Worker, Node tests and Playwright.
**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-10-pro-v7-design.md

## Global Constraints
- Arabic RTL; 44px primary controls; mobile playfields fill available width.
- Unbiased crypto dice; no bot access to opponents' private hands.
- Account and guest progress remain distinct.
- Daily dates use Asia/Riyadh; daily offer is 20% on a premium paid item.
- Retain existing online room protocol and Worker bindings.

## Review Focus
- Repeated activation during animation cannot perform two turns or two purchases.
- A bot extra turn, an empty legal move list or an empty card hand must not stall play.
- Pause and reload preserve pending rolls and avoid repeated completion rewards.
- Viewport changes and reduced motion preserve token alignment.
- Account switching cannot apply another account's daily gift or purchase.
